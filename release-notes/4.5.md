## SRT Generator 4.5 — review your subtitles before you download

**Still seeing an old screen after updating?** Quit the app with **Cmd+Q** (not just closing the window) and open it again. From 4.5 on this can't happen: the app clears its page cache after every update.

### New: Review panel

- After an SRT is made — in Song, Drama and Reels / YouTube, from lyrics or a transcript — the right column becomes a **Review panel**: the video (or waveform) with the current subtitle drawn on it, play/pause and a scrubber, and the full list of cues with their times.
- **Fix anything in place:** edit a line's text, nudge its start or end by 0.1 s or set them from the playhead, split a cue at the cursor, merge it with the next, delete it, or add a new one at the playhead. Undo and redo everything.
- **Loop this cue** to check a tricky line; **Re-time line** re-aligns just that cue after you've changed its words.
- **Warnings** for overlapping cues, cues shorter than 0.3 s and lines over the character limit, with **Fix automatically**.
- Keyboard: **Space** play/pause, **↑ ↓** move between cues, **Enter** edit.
- **Download SRT** gives your edited version (marked *Edited*); **Download original** is still there. Edits are saved in the job folder and kept if you switch pages, and words you re-type are learnt into your corrections file.

### New: Updates page

- In the sidebar: your version, when updates were last checked, a **Check for updates** button, and switches for automatic checks and automatic download-and-install.
- On Mac, **Update now** downloads, installs and relaunches in one go. A dot appears on **Updates** when a new version is out.

### Fixed

- After an update the window could keep showing the previous version's screen.
- If the old "Song SRT Generator" app is still in Applications or Downloads, you're offered (once) to move it to the Trash.

Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.5-win-lite.zip` (still untested on real Windows hardware). The Updates page opens the release page to download a new version.
