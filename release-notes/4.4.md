## SRT Generator 4.4 — a cleaner layout

### New look

- **A sidebar** replaces the tabs: Song, Drama, Reels / YouTube, then Corrections and **AI & API keys**. At the bottom you'll find the AI polish status, dark/light mode and the version. It remembers the page you last used, collapses to icons with one click, and does so on its own in narrow windows.
- **Two columns on every subtitle page:** everything you set on the left, top to bottom, and the preview, progress and result on the right, staying in view while you scroll.
- **More options:** isolation, section labels, topic words and Hinglish Fast/Accurate are tucked under one fold, which remembers whether you left it open.
- **Calmer pages:** a title and one short line, nothing more.

### AI & API keys has its own page

- Switch AI polish on or off, choose the provider, see which providers already have a saved key, and **save, test or remove** a key. The key field is masked, with a show/hide button. Keys still live only in your Mac's Keychain (Windows: Credential Manager).
- **New: Auto-polish after every transcription.** Switch it off to keep drafts offline until you click **Polish** on a draft.
- The Corrections page is now just your corrections.

### Removed

- The "Made by Aman using Claude" footer credit.

Mac: installs through the app's updater (*SRT Generator → Check for Updates…*). Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.4-win-lite.zip` (still untested on real Windows hardware).
