## SRT Generator 4.9 — History, Report a problem, and no more script lines left out

**Mac.** (The Windows installer for 4.9 follows.)

### Script lines are never silently left out any more

In 4.6–4.8, a line of your lyrics or script that the aligner wasn't sure of was dropped as "not in the audio". That was wrong too often: a sung chorus line ("Nahi toh bas,") scores very low even where it's sung, and in a long script the aligner could lose its place and drop every line after that point (one 5½-minute ad lost its last 16 lines).

Now a line the aligner isn't sure of is placed in the gap between its neighbours, and Whisper listens to that gap again (the Hinglish model for Hinglish). Spelling variants ("nahin" / "nahi") and numbers or symbols don't count as differences.

- **Heard there:** it's kept and timed in that gap ("recovered"). Nothing to do.
- **Speech there, but no clear match:** it's kept with its best timing and marked in orange: **Script line not confidently found — check it**, with ▶ Listen and Looks right.
- **No speech at all in the gap:** it's listed as **Not found in the audio**, with **Add it back** (in its gap, or at the playhead). It's in the SRT only if you add it back.

Repeated lines and choruses are matched in order, so each repeat lands on its own.

### Possible missing captions

Stretches longer than about 1.5 s where the audio has speech but no caption is on screen show in the Review panel as **Possible missing caption** (orange). Click to jump there and listen, add a caption, or mark that nothing is missing. The summary above Download counts them, and the recovered, flagged and not-found script lines.

### History

A new **History** page lists every job, newest first: date and time, file (with a thumbnail for videos), section, mode, language, duration, number of captions, status (Verified, N to check, Error) and whether you edited it. Search by file name or caption text, and filter by section, status and date.

- **Open** brings a job back into its section's Review panel exactly as you left it (video or waveform, captions, edits, flags), so you can keep editing and download the SRT or the original again.
- **Re-run** runs it again with the same settings, or opens it with everything filled in so you can change something first.
- **View log**, **Show in Finder**, **Report a problem** and **Delete** (with a confirmation).
- Settings: delete jobs older than N days (or never; never is the default), the log level (Normal / Detailed), and **Clear logs**.

### Report a problem

In the Review panel, in History, and in the sidebar for general problems (the app won't start or update). Pick what's wrong, describe it, and optionally give the time in the video (it's filled in from the playhead). Choose what to include: the job log, the captions and text, the app log from the last 24 hours, and the audio (off unless you tick it, because it's your recording). API keys are never included.

**Create report** makes a zip in your Downloads folder, shows exactly what's in it, and opens a pre-filled email for you to send. There's also **Copy report text** and **Show zip in Finder**. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

### Complete logging

Every job now keeps a detailed log (log.txt, timestamped to the millisecond) and a structured job.json: the settings, the input file, every step with its timing, every ffmpeg and separator command with its output, the models loaded, Whisper's raw output, each line's confidence, every AI request and reply, the listening check's verdict on each caption, and your edits in Review. The app keeps its own log too (app start and stop, requests, update checks, downloads, errors). Your home folder's name is replaced with "~" and keys are never written.
