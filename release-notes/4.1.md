## SRT Generator 4.1 — it learns your words

### New: Corrections

- **Every approved transcript teaches it.** The app compares Whisper's draft with the text you approved, word by word, and adds each fix to `corrections.md` — a plain Markdown file with one table per script (Hinglish, Hindi (Devanagari), Urdu, English, …): *Wrong · Correct · Times seen · Last seen*.
- **New drafts are corrected automatically** in every tab — whole words and short phrases, keeping capitals. Fixed words are **highlighted**; hover one to see what Whisper wrote and **Undo** it. The draft shows the count, e.g. "12 words auto-corrected from your corrections file".
- **Whisper gets hints**: your most frequent correct words are passed to it as hotwords, so it hears them right more often.
- **A Corrections tab** next to Song / Drama / Reels: search, edit, switch off, delete, or add corrections by hand. **Upload** teammates' `.md` files to merge them (bad rows are reported with line numbers; conflicting fixes let you choose, the one seen more often preselected), **Download corrections.md** to share yours, and **Reset** with a backup.
- Each job folder now also keeps Whisper's draft, the approved text and the word diff.

Everything stays on your Mac — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Mac: installs through the app's updater (*SRT Generator → Check for Updates…*). Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.1-win-lite.zip` (still untested on real Windows hardware).
