## SRT Generator 4.8.1 — the video stays in view while you review

**Mac.** A fix for the Review panel.

- **The video no longer scrolls away.** While a video played, the list of subtitles followed the playing line by scrolling the whole page, so the video preview moved up out of view. Now only the subtitle list scrolls; the page never moves because of playback.
- **Preview and list on screen together.** While you review, the video (or the waveform) stays pinned at the top of the right column, sized to the window, with the subtitle list in its own scroll area below, at any window size, and also when the window is narrow and the columns stack.
- **Following pauses when you need it to:** while you edit a subtitle, and for 3 seconds after you scroll the list yourself. A new **Follow playback** switch (next to *Loop this cue*, on by default) turns it off.
- Clicking a subtitle still jumps the video to that moment, without moving the page.
