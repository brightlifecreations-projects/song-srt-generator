## SRT Generator 4.7 — a real Windows app, with automatic updates

**Windows only.** The Mac app is unchanged and stays on 4.6.1; Macs keep getting their updates as before.

### New on Windows

- **Install it like any Windows app:** download `SRT-Generator-4.7-Setup.exe` and run it. No admin rights needed. It installs for you only, adds SRT Generator to the Start menu (and, if you tick the box, the desktop), and shows up under Settings → Apps for uninstalling.
  - The first time, Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"* (the app isn't from the Microsoft Store): click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Its own window.** The app opens in its own window instead of your browser. Opening it again brings that window forward, and closing the window quits the app.
- **First launch sets itself up** in a progress window, the same as on the Mac: the app's Python and packages, the AI models (about 2.5 GB, once) and a test run. Everything goes in one folder, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SRT Generator`: models, jobs, corrections and settings. Updates never download the models again.
- **Already using the zip version** (Install.bat / Start.bat)? On first launch the app offers to move that copy's models and jobs over, so nothing downloads twice. Your corrections and settings are copied too.
- **Automatic updates.** The app checks once a day (WinSparkle). When there's a new version, it downloads it, checks its signature, installs it silently and reopens the app. **Check for updates** works from the app's menu and from the Updates page, whose switches work as on the Mac.
- **Uninstalling** removes the app, then asks whether to delete your data folder too (No by default, so a reinstall needs no downloads).

The zip version (`SRT-Generator-4.7-win-lite.zip`) is still there for anyone who wants it, but it doesn't update itself.
