## SRT Generator 4.6.1 — no more flashing words, better AI line cuts

### Fixed

- **No word flashes for a millisecond.** In Word by word, every word now stays on screen for at least 0.15 s, even when the aligner gave it no time at all. The time comes from what follows, without overlapping the next word. Words the aligner left on the last instant of the file (a closing "All the best.") now use the pause before it instead of piling up at the very end. No caption runs past the end of the audio.
- **The final AI check's line cuts are used far more often.** The AI is now told where a caption must end (pauses, line ends, speaker changes, end-of-phrase punctuation). Its cuts are judged one phrase at a time, so one cut that breaks a rule no longer throws away all the good ones. The rules still hold: at most 5 words, about 30 characters, no caption ending on a weak word.
- **Hinglish is named correctly for the AI.** The final check now tells the AI the text is "Hinglish (Hindi written in Roman letters)" instead of "English". Timing still uses the English model, which is what aligns Roman-letter Hindi best.

Mac: installs through the app's updater (*SRT Generator → Check for Updates…*). Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.6.1-win-lite.zip`.
