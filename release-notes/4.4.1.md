## SRT Generator 4.4.1

- **Pick the AI model from a list.** On the AI & API keys page, the Model field is now a dropdown filled from your provider's own model list (newest first), with a refresh button. If the list can't load you get a short built-in list, and you can always type a custom model id.

Mac: installs through the app's updater. Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.4.1-win-lite.zip` (still untested on real Windows hardware).
