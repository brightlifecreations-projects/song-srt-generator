## SRT Generator 4.2 — much better Hinglish, offline

### New: a Hinglish speech model

- **Hinglish drafts are now written by a dedicated Hinglish model**: Oriserve's Whisper-Hindi2Hinglish-Apex (Apache-2.0). It writes Hindi speech straight in Roman letters, with no Devanagari step. On our test reel the draft went from 46 % of words wrong to 2 %, and on a Hindi song from 48 % to 29–30 %.
- **Much faster:** a 17-second reel takes about 8 seconds (Fast) instead of several minutes.
- **One-time download (781 MB)**, the first time you transcribe Hinglish, with a progress bar. Or click **Download now** under the script picker, or say yes when setup offers it. After that it works fully offline.
- **Fast or Accurate.** *Accurate* (the default) also gets a second opinion from the standard model, **highlights every word where the two disagree** (or where the model wasn't sure), and re-checks doubtful bits on their own. Hover a highlighted word to see why. **Use** takes the other word, **Keep** clears the highlight.
- **House spellings** for Hinglish (nahin → nahi, chaahie → chahiye, hamen → humein, …), applied to every Hinglish draft. Edit them in the **Corrections** tab.
- **Topic words:** type names, brands and terms for a job (e.g. *Lovika, reel, laptop, client*) and Whisper is told to expect them.

### Fixed

- Words from your corrections file given to Whisper as hints could make it skip those same words when they were the first thing said.

Subtitle timing is unchanged: the approved text is still aligned by the same model, which tested more precise.

Mac: installs through the app's updater (*SRT Generator → Check for Updates…*). Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.2-win-lite.zip` (still untested on real Windows hardware).
