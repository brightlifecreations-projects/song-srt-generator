## SRT Generator 4.3 — AI polish, with the provider you choose (optional)

### New: AI polish

- **An AI model fixes the words the offline draft misheard**, right after the draft, in every tab. It fixes words only: the same lines, nothing added or removed, your house spellings and corrections followed. On our Hindi song test it cut wrong words from 30 % to 24 %.
- **Use any provider:** Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible server (DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, a local Ollama or LM Studio). Set it up in **Corrections → AI polish**: pick the provider, paste your API key, **Test connection**, switch it on.
- **It's off until you add a key.** Only the transcript text is sent to the provider you choose. Audio and video never leave your computer. Keys are kept in your Mac's Keychain (Windows: Credential Manager).
- **Easy to review:** AI changes are highlighted in blue, your corrections file's fixes in green, each with the original word and one-click Undo. Words the AI isn't sure of are marked `[word?]`. **Polish again** re-runs it on your edited text.
- **If anything goes wrong** (no internet, a bad key, a rate limit, a muddled answer), your offline draft stays as it was and one line tells you why.

### Corrections learn from both

- Every learnt fix now has a **Source**: *AI*, *User*, or *AI+User* (the AI changed it and you edited it). AI fixes you undo are never learnt.
- Filter the Corrections tab by Source, or **switch off all AI rows** at once. Older corrections files without a Source column still upload and merge.

Mac: installs through the app's updater (*SRT Generator → Check for Updates…*). Windows: `SRT-Generator-4.3-win-lite.zip` (still untested on real Windows hardware).
