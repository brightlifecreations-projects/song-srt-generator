## SRT Generator 4.0

*Song SRT Generator* is now **SRT Generator** — subtitles for songs, dramas, reels and YouTube videos.

### Coming from 3.x — read this once

- Version 3 can't update itself, so its *Update available* banner brings you here **one last time**. Download `SRT-Generator-4.0-mac.zip`, drag **SRT Generator** into Applications, open it, and do **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security once.
- Quit the old app first. On its first launch 4.0 **moves** your old `Song SRT Generator` data folder: the AI models, your `jobs/` and your settings come along, so nothing big is downloaded again. The old app goes to the Trash.
- **From now on updates are automatic** (Sparkle): the app checks once a day, offers the new version, verifies its signature, installs it and relaunches. *SRT Generator → Check for Updates…* checks right away. No more downloading zips by hand.

### New

- **Three sections** — tabs at the top, each with the full feature set (I have the lyrics/script or Transcribe for me, drop or browse audio and video, vertical/horizontal, language and lyrics script including Hinglish, edit-and-approve, SRT download). Each tab remembers its own settings:
  - **Song**: isolates the vocals, one subtitle per lyric phrase, vertical by default.
  - **Drama**: isolates the dialogue from music and effects, horizontal by default, subtitles of 7 seconds at most, a new one at every pause.
  - **Reels / YouTube**: short, punchy captions (four words or less, 2.5 seconds at most). The voice is only isolated if background music is detected — or when you switch isolation on.
- **Video everywhere**: MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV and WEBM work in every tab and both modes, by dropping or browsing. A video is now previewed **with the subtitles drawn on it**.
- **Auto-updates on Mac** with Sparkle 2 (see above).

### Fixed

- MKV and M4V videos couldn't be picked in the Mac app's file browser.
- Dropping a file outside the drop box replaced the app with the file; you can now drop anywhere on the page.
- The bundled ffmpeg is always used, even when another one (or none) is on the system PATH.

### Windows

`SRT-Generator-4.0-win-lite.zip` (x64, still untested on real hardware). Windows keeps the *Update available* banner; it has no auto-update.
