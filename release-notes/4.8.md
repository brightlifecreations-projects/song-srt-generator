## SRT Generator 4.8 — every caption checked against the audio

**Mac.** (The Windows installer for 4.8 follows; Windows stays on 4.7 until then.)

### Listening check (new, offline, always on)

After your SRT is built, Whisper listens to the audio again and checks every caption against what it hears — every section, lyrics and transcribe mode, Line by line and Word by word. Nothing is sent anywhere.

- **It checks the words** (numbers and symbols don't count: "4" is "four") and **the timing** (a caption starts no earlier than about 0.2 s before its first word, ends no later than about 0.3 s after its last, never overlaps the next and is long enough to read). Hinglish is heard by the Hinglish speech model.
- **It fixes what it's sure of:** a caption timed away from its words is re-aligned to the audio, and a word the AI check changed is put back when the audio says the original.
- **It flags the rest** in the Review panel, in orange: **Check this**, with the reason (*heard ‘road’, caption says ‘zaroor’*). **▶ Listen** jumps there and plays; **Looks right** clears it, and so does editing that caption. Your own lyrics and approved transcripts are never changed by it.
- Above the download button: *Checked against the audio: 62 captions OK, 3 fixed, 1 needs your check*, with **Next to check**. When nothing is left to check, the SRT is **Verified**.
- It's its own step in the progress (*Listening check*), and **Download SRT** always gives the fixed version.

### Numbers always as digits

Every number word becomes digits: "four" → 4, "twenty five" → 25, "first" → 1st, "seven thirty" → 7:30, "seventy percent" → 70%, and Hinglish counts ("ek do teen" → 1 2 3; "do" meaning *give* stays a word). Only words that aren't numbers in context stay words — "no one", "someone", "the one", "anyone", "at first"… — and that list is editable on the **Corrections** page. A two-word number takes the start of its first word and the end of its last, so the timing stays exact.

### Clean captions

Captions keep only letters (any script, Hindi included), digits, spaces, full stops, apostrophes inside words (don't, I'm, Aditi's), % after a number and the colon in a time (7:30). Quotation marks, dashes and hyphens, commas, ? ! ; …, brackets, slashes, asterisks and emoji are removed. Punctuation still decides where captions break — the breaks are chosen first, then the symbols go. What you type in the Review panel follows the same rules, so the download always matches the list.

### A smarter final AI check (when a provider is set up)

The AI now does a full editorial pass: misheard or misspelled words, capitals and proper nouns, numbers as digits, the clean-caption rules, and line breaks by meaning (4–5 words, about 30 characters on vertical video, phrases kept whole, no line ending on a weak word, one speaker per caption). Words it isn't sure of are marked **[?]** in the Review panel (never in the SRT) — click the [?] once the word is right. Each change is checked on its own: only a change that would add or drop a spoken word, or swap in an unrelated word, is refused, and the job log says why. It still never hears the audio: only text is sent.
